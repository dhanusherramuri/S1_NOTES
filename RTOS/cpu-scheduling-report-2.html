SJF wins on average wait because it keeps clearing the shortest remaining job first, but P5 still waits 27ms under every policy here — it's the longest job and arrives last, so nothing shields it from that queue. Round robin costs the most on average because a 4ms slice against jobs this long forces several extra trips through the ready queue.
